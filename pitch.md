# CyberCash Sentinel - Video Explainer Script

**Title:** CyberCash Sentinel: Predictive Financial Cybercrime Intelligence
**Tone:** Confident, professional, and accessible (easy to understand for non-technical stakeholders).
**Duration:** ~4-5 minutes

---

## 🎬 Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)

**(Visual: Start on the main Dashboard / Live GIS Intelligence Tab)**

**Speaker:**
"Welcome to CyberCash Sentinel. Today, I'm going to walk you through our Predictive Financial Cybercrime Intelligence platform. Traditional banking security systems are reactive—they only alert you *after* the money is gone. We built CyberCash to be entirely proactive. 

Using advanced graph algorithms and machine learning, our system analyzes millions of transactions in real-time to predict and geographically pinpoint where physical cash-out attacks—like mule networks and smurfing—are going to happen *before* the money leaves the ATM."

---

## 🗺️ 1. Live GIS Intelligence (0:30 - 1:15)

**(Visual: Click on the 'GIS Intelligence' tab. Zoom in on the Threat Map. Point out the Red and Yellow ATM nodes.)**

**Speaker:**
"Let's start here on the Live GIS Intelligence map. This is the command center. 

What you're seeing on this map isn't a list of past crimes; it's a live, predictive threat map. Our AI engine continuously scans the financial network. When it detects the early stages of a coordinated attack, it highlights the physical ATM terminals that are most at risk. 

Notice the color coding: ATMs marked in **Red** indicate a 'High Risk' prediction—meaning an attack is imminent. ATMs in **Yellow** are 'Medium' or 'Low' risk. This allows law enforcement and bank security to deploy physical assets to the right location ahead of time."

---

## 🕸️ 2. Global Fraud Network (1:15 - 2:00)

**(Visual: Navigate to the 'Network' tab. Show the macro-network graph. Click on a specific Terminal Node to show the ATM Terminal Profile side-panel.)**

**Speaker:**
"Now, how does the AI know an attack is coming? Let's move to the **Global Fraud Network** tab. 

Fraudsters rarely act alone; they operate in complex networks. This interactive graph visualizes the flow of illicit funds across compromised accounts and destination terminals. 

If I click on any of these terminal nodes, a detailed **ATM Terminal Profile** slides in. It immediately gives me actionable intelligence—vault cash levels, hardware status, CCTV operational status, and the exact physical coordinates. We don't just tell you there's a threat; we give you everything you need to secure the location."

---

## 🔍 3. Incident Workspace (2:00 - 3:00)

**(Visual: Go to the 'Incidents' tab. Click on a specific High-Risk Incident to open the Incident Workspace. Cycle through the sub-tabs: Overview, Network, Predictions, and Timeline.)**

**Speaker:**
"Let's dive deeper into a specific threat. By clicking on an active incident, we enter the **Incident Workspace**. This is a dedicated dossier for a single ongoing attack.

- **Overview & Network:** We can see the isolated threat map and the specific transaction web for this cluster. 
- **Predictions Tab:** *(Click the Predictions tab)* Here, we have a live AI forecasting chart. The solid line shows how the risk probability has escalated, while the dashed line projects the threat into the future—predicting what will happen in the next 15 to 30 minutes. It's incredibly intuitive; you don't need to be a data scientist to see the risk rising.
- **Timeline Tab:** *(Click the Timeline tab)* Finally, the timeline provides a chronological ledger of every transaction, model prediction, and system alert. Notice that every prediction clearly lists the **Target Zone and Sector Radius**, ensuring there are no blind spots."

---

## 🛡️ 4. Audit & Security (3:00 - 3:45)

**(Visual: Navigate to the 'Audit' tab. Click on a specific row to open the Interactive Audit Record Dossier.)**

**Speaker:**
"Security and accountability are paramount. Let's head over to the **Audit** tab.

Every single action taken on this platform—whether an analyst acknowledges an alert, escalates it, or resolves it—is immutably logged here. 

If I click on any user action, an **Executive Action Summary** modal appears. This isn't just a basic text log. It actively pulls up the exact trigger points, the terminal IDs involved, the account numbers, and traces the funds from source to destination. It provides total transparency for compliance and post-incident forensic reviews."

---

## 📊 5. Reports & Model Operations (3:45 - 4:30)

**(Visual: Navigate to the 'Reports' tab. Click the 'Export Intelligence PDF' button and show the downloaded PDF.)**

**Speaker:**
"When it's time to brief stakeholders or law enforcement, we make reporting seamless. In the **Reports** tab, clicking the 'Export Intelligence PDF' button instantly generates a beautifully formatted, highly detailed analytical document. It captures our live key performance indicators—like Total Value at Risk and Prediction Precision—along with a ledger of the top high-risk dossiers.

*(Visual: Briefly click over to Model Ops / Simulation)*
Finally, for our technical teams, the **Model Ops** and **Adversarial Simulation** tabs allow us to monitor the AI's precision and simulate complex attacks to continually battle-test the system."

---

## 🏁 Conclusion (4:30 - 4:45)

**(Visual: Return to the main GIS Intelligence Map)**

**Speaker:**
"In summary, CyberCash Sentinel doesn't just record financial crime—it intercepts it. By combining advanced graph AI, real-time spatial intelligence, and beautiful, actionable interfaces, we are giving security teams the power to stay one step ahead of the adversaries. 

Thank you for watching."
